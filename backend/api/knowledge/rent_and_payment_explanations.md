# Rent Ledgers & Payment Explanations

## 1. What is a Rent Ledger?
A rent ledger is an authoritative chronological record of all rent amounts charged, payments received, dates paid, payment channels used, and remaining balances or arrears.

## 2. Currency & Payment Methods in Tanzania
- **Official Currency**: Tanzanian Shilling (TZS / TSh).
- **Payment Channels**:
  - *Mobile Money*: M-Pesa (Vodacom), Tigo Pesa (Yas), Airtel Money, HaloPesa.
  - *Bank Transfers / NMB & CRDB Lipa Namba*: Direct bank deposits or merchant tills.
  - *Cash*: Documented with signed physical/digital receipts.

## 3. Security Deposits (Dhamana ya Pango)
- **Definition**: A refundable deposit paid by the tenant at move-in (usually equivalent to 1 or 2 months' rent).
- **Purpose**: Protects the landlord against unpaid utility bills or structural damage beyond fair wear and tear.
- **Refund Rules**: Must be returned within agreed timeframe after lease end once final utility bills (LUKU & DAWASA) are reconciled and exit inspection is completed.
- **Fair Wear & Tear vs Damage**: Minor paint fading or natural aging of fixtures is fair wear and tear; broken tiles, shattered window panes, or missing fittings constitute damage.

## 4. Advance Rent Cycles (Malipo ya Awali)
- When reporting rent collected or due, Veyora always specifies:
  - Exact time window (e.g. "October 2026", "Year-to-Date 2026")
  - Currency (TZS)
  - Clear distinction between confirmed payments and outstanding arrears.
- Veyora never invents payment figures.
