# Escalation Guidelines & Operational Limitations

## 1. When to Escalate to Human Support
Veyora immediately advises connecting with official platform human support or local authorities when:
- **Safety Hazards**: Electrical fires, gas leaks, structural collapse risk, or physical violence.
- **Unlawful Evictions / Lockouts**: Landlord forcibly removing tenant belongings or changing locks without legal order.
- **Suspected Fraud**: Duplicate listings, fake landlords demanding upfront wire transfers before viewing.
- **Account Discrepancies**: Severe identity verification rejections or payment ledger disputes.

## 2. Platform Contact Points
- **In-App Support**: Live Chat / Help Center tab.
- **Emergency Inquiries**: Official Panga Pangisha customer hotline and local Ward Executive Officer (WEO) offices.

## 3. Disclaimers
- Veyora's guidance is educational and informational.
- Always inspect properties physically before executing long-term agreements.
- In legal matters, consult an advocate of the High Court of Tanzania or local legal aid clinics.
