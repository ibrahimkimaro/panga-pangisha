# Tanzania Housing Domain & Market Realities

## 1. Geographic Focus & Prime Hubs
- **Dar es Salaam**: The economic heart of Tanzania's rental market:
  - *Kinondoni*: Masaki, Oysterbay (upscale expatriate & diplomatic), Mikocheni, Sinza, Mwenge, Kijitonyama (popular urban residential).
  - *Ilala*: Upanga (quiet residential, central), City Centre / Posta (commercial & apartments), Kariakoo (dense commercial hub), Tabata, Segerea.
  - *Ubungo*: Kimara, Mbezi Luis, Ubungo Kibo (family-friendly, accessible along Morogoro road BRT).
  - *Temeke*: Chang'ombe, Kurasini, Mbagala (industrial & suburban).
  - *Kigamboni*: Tuangoma, Kibada, South Beach (coastal expansion, quiet living across Nyerere Bridge).
- **Other Key Regions**: Arusha (safari & NGO hub), Dodoma (government capital with high civil service rental demand), Mwanza (Lake Victoria economic zone), Zanzibar (Stone Town & coastal villas).

## 2. Property & Room Classifications
- **Standalone House (Nyumba Nzima)**: Fully detached compound with private yard, gate, and security.
- **Apartment (Ghorofa / Fleti)**: Self-contained multi-family residential units within a secured complex.
- **Self-Contained / Master Bedroom (Chumba Master)**: Room equipped with its own private internal bathroom (choo na bafu ndani).
- **Single Room (Chumba Kimoja)**: Single private bedroom sharing bathroom and kitchen facilities with other tenants on the compound.
- **Hostel**: Accommodation tailored for university students (e.g., UDSM, IFM, CBE, Ardhi).
- **Commercial (Maduka / Ofisi)**: Retail shops, office spaces, and warehouses.

## 3. National Addressing System (NAPA Postal Codes)
- Tanzania's official national addressing infrastructure assigns distinct 5-digit postal codes to every district and ward.
- Panga Pangisha integrates NAPA codes to ensure accurate physical property locations and prevent fraudulent ghost listings.

## 4. The "Dalali" Landscape vs Verified Platforms
- Traditionally, house searches relied on informal street brokers (*madalali*), who often charge upfront viewing fees (*pesa ya kuona*) and brokerage commissions (*mwezi mmoja*).
- Panga Pangisha eliminates fraudulent viewing charges by providing direct, landlord-verified listings with photos, verified amenities, and transparent pricing.
