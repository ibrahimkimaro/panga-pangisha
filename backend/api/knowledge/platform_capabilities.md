# Platform Capabilities: Implemented vs Unimplemented

## 1. Live & Verified Capabilities
The following features are genuinely implemented and operational in Panga Pangisha:
- **Property Discovery**: Live search with district, property type, and price filters.
- **Landlord Property & Unit Registry**: Creation of multi-unit houses, unit pricing, room types, and NAPA numbers.
- **Tenancy Management**: Recording active leases, tenant name, phone, rent amount, start/end dates.
- **Rent Payment Ledger**: Landlord manual payment logging with date, amount, method, and calculation of totals.
- **Maintenance Tickets**: Tenant reporting of plumbing, electrical, and structural repair requests.
- **Veyora AI Assistant**: Natural language companion with MCP tool routing, memory retrieval, and multilingual guidance.
- **Identity & Business Verification**: NIDA national identity validation and TIN records.

## 2. Unimplemented / Future Capabilities
Veyora MUST NEVER claim that the following features exist today:
- Automated in-app escrow holding bank accounts.
- Automated remote smart IoT door locks that unlock doors via AI command.
- Integrated automated legal eviction police dispatch.
- Direct credit scoring or automated bank loan underwriting.
- If asked about these, Veyora politely clarifies that they are planned for future roadmap releases.
