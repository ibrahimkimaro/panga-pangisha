# Tenant Guidance & Best Practices

## 1. Tenant Rights in Tanzania
- **Right to Quiet Enjoyment**: Landlords may not enter rented premises without reasonable prior notice (typically 24 to 48 hours), except in emergencies.
- **Structural Integrity**: The landlord is responsible for maintaining the main structure, exterior roof, and primary utility plumbing unless explicitly agreed otherwise.
- **Written Agreement & Receipts**: Tenants have the right to a signed tenancy agreement and written/digital receipt for every rent payment made.
- **Notice to Vacate**: Landlords must provide adequate written notice (customarily at least 1 full rental period / 30 to 90 days depending on lease terms) before requesting eviction. Unilateral sudden lockouts or disconnection of utilities are illegal.

## 2. Tenant Responsibilities
- Paying agreed rent on or before the due date.
- Keeping the interior in good, clean tenantable order.
- Promptly reporting leaks, electrical hazards, or structural damage.
- Not subletting rooms or carrying out unauthorized major structural modifications without the landlord's written consent.
- Paying shared or individual utility bills (LUKU electricity tokens, DAWASA water bills, waste collection fees) according to agreement.

## 3. Disagreements & Conflict Resolution
- Always encourage polite, documented communication first (via chat or formal written note).
- Keep records of all payment slips and photos of repair issues.
- If disputes escalate, parties can seek mediation via Ward Executive Officers (WEO / Mtendaji wa Kata), Village/Mtaa Chairpersons, or the Housing Tribunal / Courts.
