# Landlord Guidance & Compliance

## 1. Professional Landlord Responsibilities
- **Habitable Living Conditions**: Ensuring properties meet basic health, sanitation, water, and electrical safety standards.
- **Accurate Property Listings**: Transparently declaring monthly rent, security deposit, utility sharing rules, and restrictions (e.g. pets, commercial activities).
- **Legitimate Documentation**: Registering real tenancies with accurate tenant details, issuing clear receipts, and maintaining an organized financial ledger.
- **Security Deposit Accounting**: Holding security deposits in trust and returning them at tenancy conclusion minus legitimate, documented repair costs.

## 2. Setting Rent & Payment Cycles in Tanzania
- By custom in Tanzania, many landlords request rent payments in advance blocks of 3 months, 6 months, or 1 year (*kodi ya miezi sita / mwaka*).
- However, monthly and quarterly payments (*kodi ya mwezi / miezi mitatu*) are becoming increasingly popular in urban areas to attract reliable, salaried young professionals and families.
- Landlords on Panga Pangisha are encouraged to state payment terms clearly upfront.

## 3. Lawful Tenancy Termination & Eviction
- Disconnecting water or electricity, removing roof sheets, or locking out tenants forcibly without legal process is unlawful under Tanzanian tenancy jurisprudence.
- Formal procedure requires:
  1. Written warning notice stating breach (e.g., arrears past grace period).
  2. Statutory notice to remedy or vacate (typically 30 days).
  3. Escalation to local Ward Executive Officer (Mtendaji wa Kata) or competent court for legal eviction order if uncooperative.
