# Savings, Budgeting & Financing Guidance

## 1. The 30% Rent Budgeting Rule
- A healthy benchmark is spending no more than 30% of net monthly household income on rent and core utilities.
- For example, an individual earning TZS 1,500,000 monthly should ideally target rentals around TZS 450,000 to maintain room for savings, transport, and emergencies.

## 2. Preparing for Advance Rent Payments
- Because many Tanzanian landlords request 6 months' rent in advance, house hunters often face large lump-sum requirements.
- **Veyora's Practical Savings Advice**:
  - Open a dedicated savings pocket or mobile money lock account (e.g. M-Pesa M-Koba / Timiza).
  - Calculate upfront total: Advance Rent (e.g., 6 × TZS 300,000 = 1,800,000) + Security Deposit (300,000) + Moving costs (~150,000) = TZS 2,250,000.
  - Break into monthly savings targets over 6 or 12 months.
