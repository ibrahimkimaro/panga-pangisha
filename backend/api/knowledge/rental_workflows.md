# Rental Workflows & Step-by-Step Journeys

## 1. Renter Journey
1. **Discovery & Search**:
   - Filter by location (Region, District, Ward, NAPA), maximum rent (TZS), property type, and furnishing.
   - Inspect high-resolution photographs, verified status, and amenity tags.
2. **Scheduling Physical Viewing**:
   - Submit a viewing request specifying preferred date and time.
   - Landlord receives request and confirms appointment.
3. **Identity Verification & Agreement**:
   - Both parties verify identity (National ID / NIDA or passport).
   - Tenancy agreement details are recorded: monthly rent, advance payment term, start date, and end date.
4. **Payment & Check-in**:
   - Initial rent and security deposit payment are logged.
   - Move-in inventory checklist is documented (condition of water fittings, electricity meter initial balance, window screens).
5. **Ongoing Tenancy**:
   - Log payments, submit maintenance tickets for plumbing/electrical faults, track lease expiration.

## 2. Landlord Journey
1. **Registration & Identity Verification**:
   - Register account, submit NIDA national ID number, and provide business profile/TIN if managing commercial property.
2. **Listing Properties via 5-Step Wizard**:
   - Step 1: Basic Information (Property Name, Type, Furnishing).
   - Step 2: Location (Region, District, Ward, Street, NAPA Code).
   - Step 3: Units & Rooms (Define individual units, e.g. Room 101 Master vs Standard, specific rents).
   - Step 4: Amenities & Pricing (WiFi, parking, air conditioning, water borehole, security guard).
   - Step 5: Media & Verification (Upload photos and proof of ownership).
3. **Managing Active Rentals**:
   - View occupied vs vacant units.
   - Record rent payments and generate digital receipts.
   - Monitor and resolve maintenance requests.
